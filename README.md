Future Interns Task 3 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 3.

The objective of this project is to analyze marketing campaign data using Microsoft Power BI to understand customer subscription behavior, evaluate campaign conversion performance, and identify opportunities to improve marketing effectiveness.

The project uses the Bank Marketing Campaign dataset, which records customer responses to marketing contacts for term-deposit subscriptions.

Project Objectives

- Analyze successful and unsuccessful customer subscriptions.
- Calculate the campaign subscription conversion rate.
- Understand customer response patterns across different characteristics.
- Compare campaign outcomes by job, education, marital status, contact method, and month.
- Identify opportunities to improve marketing campaign performance.
- Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Dataset

Dataset: Bank Marketing Campaign Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographic information, campaign contact details, previous campaign interactions, and subscription outcomes.

Note: This project analyzes bank marketing campaign conversions rather than a website visitor-to-lead funnel.

Tools Used

Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total customer records
- Successful subscriptions
- Unsuccessful subscriptions
- Subscription conversion rate
- Customer age distribution
- Customer count by job
- Subscription outcomes by education
- Subscription outcomes by contact type
- Campaign performance by month
- Previous campaign contact analysis
- Marketing campaign conversion funnel
- Conversion and non-conversion rates

Key Insights

- Total customers analyzed: 41,188.
- Successful subscriptions: Approximately 4,640.
- Subscription conversion rate: 11.27%.
- Non-conversion rate: 88.73%.
- The majority of contacted customers did not subscribe, highlighting an opportunity to improve campaign effectiveness.
- Comparing customer characteristics and contact methods can help identify patterns associated with subscription outcomes.
- Reviewing campaign timing and previous contact history may help improve future marketing strategies.

Recommendations

- Focus on customer segments with higher observed subscription conversion rates.
- Review unsuccessful campaign outcomes to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Test different contact methods and campaign timings.
- Personalize marketing messages based on customer characteristics and campaign results.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of previous contact strategies before repeating campaigns.
- Monitor subscription conversion rates regularly to measure improvements.
- Use data-driven insights to improve campaign targeting and customer engagement.
